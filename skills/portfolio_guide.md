
# Portfolio Guide Skill

## Purpose

Guide the AI Agent in creating clear, authentic, and structured design portfolio case studies.

The case study should communicate the designer's thinking, process, design decisions, and final outcome without inventing information.

## Case Study Structure

Organize the case study into relevant sections based on the available project information:

1. Project Overview
2. Context and Background
3. Problem or Design Opportunity
4. Research and Insights
5. Design Goals
6. Design Process and Iterations
7. Key Design Decisions
8. Final Solution
9. Outcome and Reflection

Only include sections that are relevant to the project and supported by the available information.

## Writing Guidelines

- Write concisely.
- Explain the reasoning behind design decisions.
- Focus on the designer's process, not only the final product.
- Use specific evidence from the provided project materials.
- Do not invent research findings, user feedback, metrics, or outcomes.
- Do not present assumptions as confirmed facts.
- Maintain a professional but natural tone.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon and overly complicated language.

## Analysis Guidelines

Identify the following when the information is available:

- Project context
- Problem or opportunity
- Intended users
- Research methods
- Research insights
- Design constraints
- Process and iterations
- Design decisions
- Final solution
- Outcomes and reflections

Connect design decisions to the problems, goals, or insights that informed them.

## Missing Information

When important information is missing:

1. Identify what is missing.
2. Do not fill the gap with assumptions.
3. Ask a specific, targeted question.
4. Continue using the information that is available.

## Authenticity Check

Before producing the final case study:

- Check whether claims are supported by the retrieved material.
- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation.
- Flag unclear or contradictory information.
- Do not claim that the designer completed work that is not documented.
- Represent the designer's actual contribution accurately.

## Output

Produce a structured case study content draft with clear headings.

If important information is missing, include targeted questions instead of inventing content.
